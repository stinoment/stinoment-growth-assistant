# Stinoment Growth Assistant

A free WordPress plugin that checks your site every day, tells you in plain words what to fix, and fixes it in 1 click.

Made by STINOMENT, an IT agency that has helped hundreds of websites grow. Current version: 1.3.1.

## The problem

Every site owner asks the same questions. Why did my traffic drop? Which pages are close to page one on Google? Is something broken that I cannot see? The answers hide in Google Search Console, many tabs, and charts that were never written for normal people. Stinoment Growth Assistant puts the answers in one place, in simple words.

## What it does

**A daily check-up with 50+ checks.** It looks at security, site health, SEO, content, links, speed, WooCommerce and comments. Every problem shows where it is, why it matters and how to fix it.

**One-click fixes, always undoable.** Nothing changes until you click. Every change is saved in a history with before and after, so you can go back. Many fixes run in small batches: image alt text, H1 to H2, category descriptions, and redirects for broken links.

**Google Search Console and Bing in plain words.** See clicks, impressions and position. Find pages near page one, searches with no clicks, pages that lose traffic, and more. There are 4 simple ways to connect Google, each with a step-by-step guide.

**A 27-day growth plan.** Weekly tasks that tick themselves off, with before and after numbers and a growth score. It is free for everyone. Each day you also see the 3 tasks that help most.

**An AI assistant.** Ask questions about your own site and get answers based on your check-up. Make better Google titles and descriptions, and FAQs for your posts. You see a preview first, and you can undo.

**Speed test.** A letter grade from A to F, Core Web Vitals, and the plugin or theme that makes a page heavy. Get a weekly test that warns you about slowdowns.

**Images.** One-click alt text, lighter copies of big images, and WebP conversion. Your originals stay safe.

**Security.** Login protection, a private login URL, two-step login, file checks against the WordPress.org originals, and alerts by email, Telegram or webhook.

**Backups.** Database backups or full backups, built in small steps for shared hosting.

**Backlinks and competitors.** See who links to you, find lost links and fix them with redirects. Watch up to 5 competitor sites.

**Toolbox.** A redirect manager, safe editing of robots.txt, llms.txt and .htaccess, SMTP email, a cookie banner with Google Consent Mode v2, and checks for AI bot access.

**For online shops.** WooCommerce checks and revenue leak alerts: failed payments, unpaid checkouts and best sellers that are out of stock.

**Monthly report.** A PDF report you can print or send by email.

Free sites get 1 AI action every 24 hours. All other features are free. If you want more, Premium adds more AI actions, social posts for each post and product, article ideas from your own search data, and a check before you publish.

## Who it is for

Business owners, bloggers, online shop owners, freelancers and agencies. You do not need to know SEO. It works in English and Persian, and it needs no account.

## How to get it

Open your WordPress dashboard, go to Plugins, then Add New, and search for **Stinoment Growth Assistant**. Install it and run the check-up.

You can also search **Stinoment Growth Assistant** on Google to find the plugin page. To reach our website, search **Stinoment**.
