# Stinoment Growth Assistant

**A free WordPress plugin that tells you, in plain words, why your traffic moves and what to fix this week.**

Made by [STINOMENT](https://stinoment.com/), an IT agency that has helped hundreds of websites grow.

## The problem

Every site owner asks the same questions. Why did my traffic drop? Which pages are close to page one on Google? Is something broken that I cannot see? Finding the answer usually means Google Search Console, many tabs, and charts that were never written for normal people.

## What it does

**It explains your data.** It reads your Google Search Console and Bing data and explains it in simple sentences.

**It checks your site every day.** More than 50 checks: titles, headings, broken links, missing pages, images without alt text, speed, security, email delivery and WooCommerce shops.

**It gives you a short to-do list.** Each task shows the time it needs and the impact it can have.

**It fixes problems with one click.** Nothing changes until you click, and every change can be undone from a history with before and after.

**It prepares you for AI search.** It shows which AI robots can read your site, so you decide who gets in.

## Who it is for

Business owners, bloggers, online shop owners, freelancers and agencies. You do not need to know SEO. Every finding explains what it means and why it matters.

## How to get it

Open your WordPress dashboard, go to Plugins, then Add New, and search for **Stinoment Growth Assistant**. Install it and run the check-up.

You can also search **Stinoment Growth Assistant** on Google to find the plugin page. To reach our website, search **Stinoment**.

Plugin page: https://wordpress.org/plugins/stinoment-growth-assistant/
